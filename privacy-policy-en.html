Summary

The app “My Koi Pond” does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no servers run by the developer. The app does not need an internet permission and therefore cannot connect to the internet on its own.

What data the app stores on your device

Everything you enter in the app is stored only locally on your device:

  • Water values and measurement data
  • Pond data (volume, depth, filter)
  • Entries in the care log
  • Koi collection (name, variety, length, year of purchase, note, growth history and optionally a photo)
  • Contacts (name, category, phone number, email address, website, note)
  • Settings (measurement interval, reminder, colour scheme, language)

This data does not leave your device. The developer has no access to it.

Storage location and protection

The data is stored in your device's protected app storage. Other apps normally cannot access it. The app does not encrypt the data additionally. We therefore recommend a screen lock and device encryption, which is usually active on current Android devices.

Data of other people

If you enter contacts of dealers, vets or other people, you store their data on your own device. Only enter data that you are allowed to use for this purpose.

Permissions

The app only needs these permissions:

  • Notifications (optional, only if you turn on the measurement reminder)
  • Receiving the device restart (so that a scheduled reminder is restored after the device restarts)

The app does not ask for access to the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone or storage.

Photos

When you add a photo, the app opens your device's image picker. The app only gets access to the image you select. A reduced-size copy is stored locally in the app; the original remains unchanged.

Reminders

When you turn on the reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device for the due date of the next measurement. This happens locally and without a server. The scheduled time is stored in the app storage so that it can be restored after a restart.

Phone, email and websites

If you tap a phone number, an email address or a website in a contact, the phone app, your email app or your browser opens. This only happens when you tap yourself. When you open a website, the privacy policy of the browser and the site applies. The app itself does not transmit any data in the process.

Feedback and support

If you give feedback or contact support using the buttons in the settings, your email app opens with a prepared message. You send it yourself. It only contains what you write. The developer processes your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you and deletes them when they are no longer needed. The app itself does not transmit any data in the process. Please do not include any details in the message that are not necessary.

Data backup

In the settings you can back up your data as a file or as text. The backup contains your data, including contacts, and photos if you choose. You decide where to store it or who to give it to, for example in a cloud, by email or in a note. In the Android app you choose the storage location in the Android dialog yourself, and when loading, the app only sees the file you select. From the moment you pass it on, the privacy policy of the respective service applies. Keep the backup safe, because it is not encrypted. The app does not send the backup anywhere itself.

Android system functions

The interface of the app is displayed with the Android system component WebView. The pages are stored in the app itself; nothing is loaded from the internet. If the Android data backup of your device is active, Android may back up app data as part of your Google account. You control this in the Android settings.

Google Play

If you install the app via Google Play, Google processes data according to its own privacy policy, for example your account and installation data. The developer has no influence on this. Google may provide the developer with anonymised statistics, for example on installations or crashes. Crash reports only exist if you have allowed the sharing of diagnostic data in Android.

Storage period and deletion

The data remains stored until you delete it. You delete entries individually in the app. All data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in the Android settings. You delete backup files and copies in other services yourself.

Children

The app is aimed at adults (18 years and older).

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since the developer does not receive any data from you, you can exercise these rights directly in the app and on your device. If you have questions, write to the email address given above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For non-public bodies in Bavaria, the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (www.lda.bayern.de) is responsible. You can also contact the authority at your place of residence.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when the app gets new features. You can find the current version in the app under Settings and in the Play Store.
